Where do we find good deals?

The purchase price makes a big difference.

We use our network, off-market opportunities and our own monitoring software to find Budapest apartments below market value.

  • Off-market opportunities

    Through estate-agent contacts, inherited-property sales and building managers, we also access apartments that have not yet appeared on listing sites.

  • A stronger position with cash

    Available capital lets us buy without arranging a mortgage. A quick, predictable agreement is valuable to sellers and helps us negotiate a below-market purchase price.

  • Our own software monitors the market

    Alongside our network, our own software continuously monitors property listings and alerts us to good buying opportunities, helping us respond quickly.

You do not need to browse listings or chase estate agents. Finding the right purchase and negotiating the price are part of our work.

How is this calculated?

Net project profit = sale price − purchase price − renovation − other costs. Your income is 50% of net project profit, with a minimum of 3% of total funding. Any shortfall is shown as funding required from Urbanflip. Project return = your income / total funding × 100. Annualised return = ((1 + project return / 100)^(12 / months) − 1) × 100. Taxes are not deducted.

What if I completed two projects?

We assume two consecutive projects with the same percentage investor result, reinvesting the entire closing amount. Second-project costs scale proportionally. This is a calculation assumption, not a promise of an achievable return.

The essentials before we start

When you get paid and how we settle up

  • The property and your money

    The property is registered in your name or your company’s name. The minimum investment is HUF 40–50 million, depending on the project, funding purchase, renovation and associated costs. You can also pay contractor invoices directly.

  • What exactly do we split?

    We deduct the purchase price, renovation, furnishing, acquisition, legal, holding and selling costs from the sale price. We split the remaining net project profit before your own taxes.

  • When are you paid?

    At settlement after the sale, you receive your capital plus profit. The total timeline includes the search, renovation and sale. A longer sale period also delays payment.

  • As a private individual

    Taxable income from a property sale is generally subject to 15% personal income tax. Regular business-like flipping may involve different rules and additional taxes; we clarify this with your accountant at the start.

    NAV: taxation of property sales

  • You can invest through a company

    You do not need to withdraw capital personally from your company first: the company can buy the property. Urbanflip can invoice the company for its fee. Taxation and deductibility depend on the legal form, tax regime and transaction.

  • Timing, guarantee and exit

    Demand affects when the apartment sells. Capital repayment and the 3% guaranteed profit are Urbanflip’s contractual commitment, not a bank or government guarantee; performance also depends on our ability to pay. Force majeure and early exit are defined in the agreement.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

How do you find apartments at attractive prices?

We use several sources: estate-agent contacts, inherited-property sales and building managers also give us access to off-market opportunities. Buying with available cash strengthens our negotiating position and helps us buy below market value. Our own software continuously monitors listings and alerts us to good deals. We handle the search and price negotiation.

Who owns the apartment?

It is registered in your name. You own the property; we find the apartment and organise the renovation and sale. If you invest through a company, the company owns it.

Do I really just provide the capital?

Yes. We handle daily work: finding the property, design, contractors, procurement, furnishing and sale. No chasing trades or visiting the site. We will need your signature or authorisation for the necessary agreements.

How do we split the net profit?

We deduct the purchase price and all accounted project costs from the sale price: renovation, furnishing, acquisition, legal, holding and selling costs. Half the remaining net project profit is yours, half is ours. Here, net means after project costs but before your own taxes.

What is the minimum investment?

The minimum investment is HUF 40–50 million, depending on the project. It funds the purchase, renovation and associated costs. At our first conversation we work out which type of project suits your budget.

Is there a profit guarantee?

Yes: we guarantee 3% profit per project on your full, documented project funding. At settlement after the sale you are entitled to your capital plus at least 3%. If half the net profit is higher, you receive the higher amount. The 3% is included in your profit share; the guarantee applies per project, not per year. With HUF 50 million, the guaranteed profit is HUF 1.5 million before your own taxes.

What if renovation costs rise or the apartment sells for less?

Our 3% profit guarantee still applies. If the sale does not cover the full invested capital plus the guaranteed profit, Urban Flip Studio funds the difference at settlement. You do not have to solve the daily problems: we manage the project and contractors.

When do I receive my money back?

At settlement after the apartment is sold, together with your return. Allow time for the search, renovation and sale. A delayed sale also delays payment; the 3% profit guarantee applies to the project, not to a fixed annual period.

What risks should I consider?

The main uncertainty is timing: weaker demand may make the sale take longer. Capital repayment and the guaranteed profit are Urban Flip Studio’s contractual commitment, not a bank or government guarantee; performance also depends on our ability to pay. Force majeure and early exit are addressed in the contract.

How are private individuals and companies taxed?

For a private individual, taxable income from a property sale is generally subject to 15% personal income tax. Regular business-like flipping may involve different rules and additional taxes, so your accountant should check your position at the start. Company taxation depends on legal form and tax regime. You do not need to withdraw capital personally first: the company can buy the apartment and Urban Flip Studio can invoice the company for its fee. Yes. You can pay contractor invoices directly while we still organise and manage the work. We agree the schedule and budget in advance.
