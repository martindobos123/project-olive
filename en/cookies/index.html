Measurement and consent

Optional measurement relies on your prior consent for each purpose (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Analytics measures visits, traffic sources, CTA and contact clicks, form usage and error categories. Marketing measures ads and supports advertising audiences. Rejecting it does not restrict use of the site.

Measurement providers may process technical data (IP address, browser, page address, time, cookie or device identifiers) and event data. Our events exclude form values, calculator amounts, email addresses, phone numbers and error message text. Advertising platforms may combine data with their account information and use it for profiling; the website makes no automated decisions with legal effects.

Google Tag Manager loads tags only after consent for the configured measurement purposes. Configured providers are listed below.

  • Google Analytics 4 — Google Ireland Limited — _ga, _ga_*: 180 nap / days

These periods concern the listed measurement cookies; the linked lists describe additional cookies on provider domains. Data may be transferred outside the EEA; the provider notices identify recipients and transfer safeguards. Analytics event data is retained for 2 months and user data for 14 months; new activity may reset user-data retention. Aggregated reports may have separate provider retention periods.

Change or withdraw your choice anytime using “Measurement settings” in the footer. Withdrawal stops measurement, clears accessible first-party measurement cookies and reloads the page. Clear cookies on provider domains through your browser. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Local storage

The ufs-consent-v2 local storage record contains your choice, time and version for up to 180 days. It is needed to remember the choice and is not a visitor identifier. Provider changes require a new choice. Only after analytics consent, filtered UTM parameters, landing page and referrer domain are stored in ufs-attribution-v1 session storage; it expires after 30 minutes and is cleared when the tab closes.

Images and fonts are hosted with the site. Calculator values stay in page memory. Forms prepare an email draft and cannot confirm that it was sent.
