Project facts

Location
Gőzmozdony utca, Budapest
Scope
Complete renovation and interior fit-out
Floor area

Build time

Starting point

Before the renovation the apartment was completely emptied and partly stripped: old flooring and tiles, exposed service risers and worn-out doors and windows.

The goal

A bright, coherent and easy-to-use home — with a new kitchen, bathroom and WC and a calm, warm colour palette.

    New tiling, bathtub and vanity, and the service riser concealed in the WC.

  • Living spaces

    Continuous flooring, fresh walls and restrained, warm-toned furnishings.
