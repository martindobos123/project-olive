How we would work together

Your capital. Our coordination.

A flip means purchasing, renovating and reselling an apartment. In this model you provide funding and we run the project; the details are agreed together before starting.

  1. Scope and agreement

    Capital, costs, decision-making, risks and settlement: first we agree the rules.

  2. Search and purchase

    We search for a Budapest apartment within the project scope. The purchased property is registered in your name.

  3. Design and renovation

    We organise design, construction and furnishing against the agreed budget.

  4. Sale

    We prepare and organise the sale. Price and time to sell also depend on market conditions.

  5. Settlement and profit sharing

    We account for project costs. The remaining positive project profit is shared equally.

Risks are part of the picture.

Sale price, costs and duration may change; losses are possible. Minimum-return terms are not yet final and this page provides no return guarantee. Decision-making, overruns, exit and settlement must be defined in an individual agreement.

How is this calculated?

Project result = sale price − purchase price − renovation − other costs. For a positive result, the investor receives 50%. Project return = investor result / total funding × 100. In a loss scenario, this stress example assigns all losses to the investor; this is not a contractual allocation. No minimum return is included.

What if I completed two projects?

We assume two consecutive projects with the same percentage investor result, reinvesting the entire closing amount. Second-project costs scale proportionally. This is a calculation assumption, not a promise of an achievable return.

Questions and answers

What to clarify before starting.

Who owns the apartment?

Under the proposed model, the apartment is registered in your name. You fund the purchase and renovation; Urban Flip Studio organises the project. Ownership alone does not protect against a fall in property value.

What does Urban Flip Studio do?

We search for a suitable Budapest apartment, plan and organise the renovation, then organise the sale. We handle daily operational decisions; steps requiring your signature or authorisation and decision-making powers are defined in the contract.

How is the shared profit calculated?

The purchase price and contractually agreed project costs are deducted from sale proceeds. These may include renovation, furnishing, acquisition, legal, holding and selling costs. The remaining positive project profit is split equally. Personal taxes and the allocation of losses must be clarified separately.

How much capital is needed?

The initial budget discussed is approximately HUF 40–50 million and upwards, covering the purchase and renovation together. Actual funding depends on the property, associated costs and contingency. An enquiry does not commit you to invest.

How long is the capital committed?

The project runs from the property search through renovation to the completed sale and settlement. We do not promise a fixed sale date. The calculator duration is an assumption, not a commitment; early exit terms must be agreed in advance.

What if costs rise or the sale price falls?

Higher costs and a lower sale price reduce the result; a loss is possible. Contingency, overruns, additional funding and allocation of losses must be clarified before starting. The calculator also shows loss scenarios.

Is there a minimum return?

We are planning a 3% minimum return per project, but the commitment and contractual terms are still being finalised. This is not currently a return guarantee. The calculator assumes no guaranteed payout: it illustrates the entered project figures, including potential losses.

Can I pay contractors directly?

This is possible in the model discussed: you may pay contractor invoices directly. Invoicing, payment schedules and eligible costs must be agreed before the project starts. The individual agreement defines the cost structure.

Do the projects demonstrate investor returns?

The Vámház körút, Gőzmozdony utca and Balázs Béla utca case studies show completed renovation work with our own photos. No investor returns or sale profits are stated, as supporting financial accounts are not provided here.
