2. Enquiries and requests for a quote

We process your name, email, any phone number supplied and message. Renovation and sale-preparation enquiries may include property location, size, condition, listing URL, service, budget and timing; investor enquiries may include planned capital and start date. Supply only information needed for the enquiry; do not send identity documents or other people’s personal data.

Purpose: the discussion, response and quote you requested. Enquiries about your own prospective contract rely on GDPR Article 6(1)(b). General enquiries or business enquiries on someone else’s behalf rely on the legitimate interest in correspondence and responding, under Article 6(1)(f); you may object.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required for the selected form; without them it cannot be submitted. Other fields are optional and you may instead contact us by email or telephone. Providing data is not a statutory requirement. Acknowledging this notice is not consent to newsletters or advertising.

Enquiries that do not result in a contract are kept for up to 12 months after closure, only as needed for follow-up under legitimate interests. Active enquiries are retained until discussions close. In a dispute, only necessary records are retained until the dispute and applicable claim period end, based on the legitimate interest in legal claims. Contract-related processing will be explained separately before a contract is concluded.

3. Forms and communications

All forms — renovation, investor and sale-preparation — prepare an email in your own email application. Only sending it there delivers it to the Gmail mailbox above. There is no external form relay. Enquiry messages are not added to the website’s public source code.

Email: Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), with affiliates involved under Google’s service terms. Google’s privacy policy describes its own controller activities. Your chosen email provider handles sending under its own terms.

Access is limited to what is necessary to handle enquiries. We do not sell enquiry data and an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. Disclosures to courts or authorities are made where legally required.

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical connection data to serve and secure the site. We do not build visitor profiles. We do not configure the provider’s log retention; GitHub’s privacy statement describes its own processing.

WhatsApp and Instagram links lead to external services; there are no embedded social widgets. Opening a link connects you to that service. WhatsApp: privacy policy. Messages sent there are handled for the enquiry purposes above.

You may request details of transfers and copies of safeguards using the email above.

6. Cookies, calculators and automated decisions

There is no active analytics or advertising tracker in the current configuration. Calculators run locally without submitting values; there is no automated decision-making or profiling about individuals. See the cookie notice.

7. Your rights and our response

You may request access and a copy, correction, and erasure or restriction where GDPR conditions apply. You may object to legitimate-interest processing on grounds relating to your situation. Portability applies to data you supplied that is processed automatically on consent or contract grounds. Send requests to the email above. We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. Complex or numerous requests may require up to two additional months; we explain any extension within the first month. Requests are normally free; proportionate identity verification may be needed.
