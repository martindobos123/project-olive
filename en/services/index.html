Services

Your home or an investment — we manage the project

We help renovate, design and furnish your own apartment. If you want to invest without managing the property search or renovation, we handle the project from purchase through to sale.

Full-scope apartment renovation

From demolition to the handover of the finished apartment, we organise and manage the entire build. We coordinate the trades, keep track of the schedule and make sure the technical solutions match the design.

The work is carried out by specialists and trades suited to the project; organisation, coordination and communication all run through us.

Sale preparation and home staging

We fund the agreed tidying, refresh and furnishing to make your apartment more appealing to buyers. You pay commission after the sale from the proceeds.

Full renovation and substantial upgrades are separate, owner-funded projects. Buyers can purchase the supplied furnishings or we take them back.

Partial renovation and refresh

Not every apartment needs a complete overhaul. A new kitchen, a bathroom, new flooring, paint or lighting can change a lot — in these cases we build on what is already there.

Investor partnership

We find a Budapest apartment, buy it in your name, renovate it and sell it. You fund the purchase and renovation; we manage the entire project. After accounted project costs, we split the net profit 50–50. The apartment stays in your ownership until the sale.

You do not need to search listings, coordinate trades or visit the site. Our dedicated investor page covers the terms, return guarantee, calculator and investment case studies.

Renovation process

How a renovation works

We agree on what you want, the budget and the schedule before work starts. Then we organise the trades and keep track of the work.

  1. Consultation and brief

    We talk through your plans for the apartment, how you would use it and your budget. That helps us work out what needs changing.

  2. Site survey and quote

    We survey the technical condition and features of the apartment, then prepare a quote based on what we find.

  3. Design and material selection

    Layout, lighting, finishes, sanitaryware, kitchen and built-in furniture — the decisions are made before construction starts.

  4. Construction and coordination

    We coordinate and lead the trades, track the schedule and make sure the build follows the design.

  5. Furnishing, styling and handover

    Furniture, textiles, lighting and the finishing touches go in, then we walk through the finished apartment together.
